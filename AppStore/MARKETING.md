# Ahar — offline foodIQ / AharIQ

**Know what you eat. No account. No internet.**

Ahar is an iPhone and iPad app for looking up foods, spices, ingredients, and everyday dishes. Nutrition, serving calculations, and health notes run on the device as foodIQ — AharIQ.

## Why Ahar

- Search by English names or everyday names such as anar, haldi, or manjal
- Nutrition per 100 g, then scale to the amount you are eating
- Curated health notes where the bundled data includes them
- Favorites and recently viewed, stored only on your phone
- Works in Airplane Mode
- No ads, no tracking, no sign-in

## Not a medical device

Ahar provides general food information for education. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

## Get the app

Search **Ahar** on the App Store (iOS 17 or later).

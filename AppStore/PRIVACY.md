# FoodSense Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 29 August 2026

FoodSense is an offline-first Indian food and nutrition reference app. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Host this file (or `../PRIVACY.md`) on a public HTTPS page and use that URL in App Store Connect.

## Summary

FoodSense does **not** collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporters, and no internet requirement.

Apple App Store privacy nutrition label: **Data Not Collected**.

## Data that stays on this device

The following stay in the app’s local storage (SwiftData) on your device and never leave the device:

- Bundled food and nutrition records imported from JSON that ships inside the app
- Foods you mark as favorites
- Recently viewed foods
- Quantity values you enter while looking at a food

Uninstalling the app deletes this on-device data.

## What we do not do

- No user accounts or sign-in
- No name, email, phone number, or payment collection
- No location tracking
- No HealthKit or medical records access
- No advertising identifiers (IDFA) or tracking
- No analytics or usage telemetry
- No third-party SDKs for ads, crash reporting, or social login
- No network requests by the app
- No cloud sync

## Tracking

FoodSense does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. The privacy manifest sets tracking to false and lists no tracking domains.

## Health information

Nutrition and health text in the app is general food information for education. It is **not** medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is not a substitute for a qualified health professional.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children. There is no social, chat, or account feature.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date will change. Because the app does not collect contact information, we cannot email you.

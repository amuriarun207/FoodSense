## How Ahar works

1. Open Ahar. On first launch the app imports bundled food data onto the device. This does not need Wi-Fi or cellular.
2. Search a food by English name or an everyday name (for example **anar**, **haldi**, **manjal**).
3. Open a food to see nutrition per 100 g, change the quantity, and read health notes when they are included.
4. Tap the heart to save a favorite. Recently viewed foods appear on Home.
5. Open Settings for data version, evidence labels, and the not-medical-advice note.

Everything above works in Airplane Mode.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Ahar need the internet?**  
No. Search, nutrition, favorites, and recently viewed stay on this device.

**Do I need an account?**  
No. There is no sign-in.

**Why do some foods say nutrition is not available?**  
V1 ships a starter dataset. Some records are labeled demo and do not include a full nutrient panel yet. A larger curated set can ship in a later update.

**Is Ahar medical advice?**  
No. Ahar is general food information for education. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. It is not a substitute for a qualified health professional.

**How do I delete my data?**  
Uninstalling Ahar removes on-device favorites, recently viewed foods, and local storage. We do not hold a copy.

**I found a nutrition value that looks wrong.**  
Email [arunkumar6207@gmail.com](mailto:arunkumar6207@gmail.com) with the food name and what you expected. We will review the bundled data.

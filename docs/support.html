How to use Ahar

  1. Open Ahar. First launch imports bundled food data onto the device. Wi-Fi is not required.
  2. Search a food by English name or an everyday name (for example anar, haldi, manjal).
  3. Open a food for nutrition per 100 g, change the quantity, and read health notes when they are included.
  4. Tap the heart to save a favorite. Recently viewed foods appear on Home.
  5. Open Settings for data version and the not-medical-advice note.

All of this works in Airplane Mode.

Frequently asked questions

Does Ahar need the internet?

No. Search, nutrition, favorites, and recently viewed stay on this device.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in.

Why do some foods say nutrition is not available?

Version 1.0 ships a starter dataset. Some records are labeled demo and do not include a full nutrient panel yet.

Is this medical advice?

No. Ahar is general food information for education. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling Ahar removes on-device favorites, recently viewed foods, and local storage. We do not keep a copy.
